Research

I am currently working on two main strands of research: (1) Social media and well-being — examining how social media use, and addictive use in particular, relates to loneliness and well-being; and (2) Political behavior — studying affective polarization, political stereotypes, and how political attitudes shape intergroup relations in contemporary societies. Methodologically, I rely on quantitative approaches, especially longitudinal data and experiments, to identify causal processes underlying these phenomena.
